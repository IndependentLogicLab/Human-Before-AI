# Phase 1 — Preliminary Baseline Report

**Research organization:** IndependentLogicLab

**Project:** Human Before AI

**Date:** November 24, 2026

## Objective

Evaluate whether GPTZero incorrectly classifies historical human-authored publications as AI-generated.

## Dataset

- Publication: On the Origin of Species
- Author: Charles Darwin
- Publication year: 1859
- Total samples prepared: 8
- Total words collected: 11,606
- Detector version: 2026-11-04-base

## Preliminary Results

- Samples tested: 7
- Words tested: 10,500
- Correct human classifications: 7
- False positives: 0
- Mean reported AI probability: 0%
- Pending samples: 1
- Research expenditure: $0.00

## Limitations

- All samples originate from one publication.
- Results cannot establish general detector accuracy.
- Complete historical text verification remains pending.
- Free-tier scanning limits prevented completion.
- API access remains pending.
- Potential overlap with detector training data is unknown.

## Phase 2 Research Hypothesis

Historical procedural and technical writing may produce different classification outcomes from conventional historical prose.

## Phase 2 Objectives

1. Expand the dataset across ten publication categories.
2. Increase author and publication diversity.
3. Prepare an initial dataset of 500 samples.
4. Preserve original historical wording.
5. Record all detection outcomes objectively.
6. Investigate and independently verify potential false positives.

## Conclusion

GPTZero correctly classified all seven tested historical passages. No false positives or bounty candidates have been identified.

Further investigation is required.
